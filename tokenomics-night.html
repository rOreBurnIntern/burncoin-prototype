The recycle loop

two of the four stages are live figures
01

Sinks

Fuel sales, upgrades, mint fees and the Crucible take $BURN out of players’ hands.

Buyback

Revenue in RON is routed to $BURN through the untaxed wBURN pool.

04

Emission

on-chain

42,066.6

$BURN a day back to miners — flat rate, no halving.

Nothing in this loop mints new supply. Every $BURN a miner is paid has already been counted in circulating supply since the day the cap was minted — the reserve moves into the float, it does not grow the float. That is what makes the emission non-inflationary against the hard cap.
