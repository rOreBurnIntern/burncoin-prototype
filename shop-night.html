view on Ronin

20 miner units are listed across 7 of the 30 tier × level combinations. The other 23 have no open order right now: Ember L2 L3 L4 · Flame L2 L3 L4 · Inferno L1 L2 L3 L4 · Pyro L1 L2 L3 L4 · Volcano L1 L2 L3 L4 · Hellfire L1 L2 L3 L4 L5. Prices are RON at the cheapest open ask; the $BURN equivalent adds a 15% slippage allowance for the BURN → RON conversion, and any RON left over after the purchase is refunded to you.

A range like 100.00 RON is one order, not a market. Several of these levels have a single unit on the book, so the cheapest ask can sit well above or below where the tier actually trades — read the order count beside it before treating a floor as a price.

Buy with locked $BURN

Move locked $BURN into the shop wallet and buy without paying the withdrawal tax. It converts to RON at purchase and refunds the remainder.

What a rig needs

Every rig needs its lock posted (2,500 – 25,000 $BURN) and fuel every day (1 – 32 units). Buying the NFT is the cheap half.

Where the money goes

Fuel sales are split: part buys $BURN back into the rewards reserve, part funds the treasury that pays the Friday dividend.

Connect a Ronin wallet to buy, and to spend locked $BURN.

The book, the prices and the mint status above are public and already current.
